Wellbeing Self-Check

24 short statements about your last two weeks at work. Your answers build a profile across the four dimensions of Te Whare Tapa Whā and point you to the training that fits you best.

This is a reflection tool, not a clinical assessment. It takes about 4 minutes.

Your wellbeing profile

Scores run from 1 to 10. A higher score means that area needs more attention.
